# Personal Ethical Observations

*A Solarpunk World Seed*

---

## Purpose

Personal Ethical Observations is an open-source journaling application dedicated to the lifelong practice of ethical reflection.

It began as a fork of an Uta Hagen-inspired journaling project and respectfully builds upon that foundation to explore a different question:

> **Who am I becoming?**

Rather than attempting to determine what is morally correct, this project encourages individuals to observe, record, and evaluate their own decisions through voluntary self-reflection.

The software is intended to act as a mirror—not a judge.

---

## Philosophy

This project is founded on several ideas.

- Character can be cultivated through deliberate practice.
- Reflection becomes more meaningful when it is consistent.
- Ethical growth belongs to the individual.
- Software should assist reflection rather than replace conscience.
- Open-source collaboration can create tools that strengthen communities.

---

## Current Vision

The journal will gradually evolve from a traditional diary into a structured ethical notebook.

Possible capabilities include:

- Daily reflections
- Decision journaling
- Self-selected virtues
- Voluntary self-ratings
- Personal principles
- Ethical observations over time
- Personal constitution builder
- Progress visualization
- Markdown export

---

## Design Principles

This project intentionally avoids assigning moral scores.

Instead, users are invited to evaluate themselves according to the values they personally choose to cultivate.

The application records observations.

The human provides the judgment.
